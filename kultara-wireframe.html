Kultara — Lo-Fi Wireframes

Story-led, location-based cultural heritage exploration. 15 screens covering the core loop: discover on map → preview quest → travel & check in → live story steps → collect → journal.

Revised flow

Changes from the sticky-note flow, and why:

  • Onboarding now carries the permission asks (location + notifications) with a reason shown. Location is the whole product — asking cold at the map is where installs die.
  • Auth gains "Explore as guest." Research said travellers want to look before committing. Account is required only at first quest start, so signup happens when motivation is highest.
  • Map / Journal / Profile become a tab bar, not a linear chain. They are peers, always reachable.
  • "Location check" is not a screen — it's a state inside the active quest (Travelling → Arrived → Step). Made it a live screen with distance + "I'm here" fallback for bad GPS.
  • Added quest completion + collectible reward. The original flow ended mid-quest; the payoff (souvenir stamp, journal entry, share) is what drives the loop back to the map.
  • Notification is two things: a proximity push ("you're 200m from a historically significant place") that deep-links to the quest preview, plus an in-app notification list.

Notes for hi-fi

  • Visual direction: warm sandstone / volcanic dark, one hot accent (temple gold or saffron). Editorial serif for story text, clean sans for UI — the story screens should read like a book, not a game HUD.
  • Offline first: quest content downloads at start; the app must survive losing signal mid-quest.
  • Respect at sites: a short etiquette card (dress, restricted areas) before the first stop of any temple quest — this came straight out of the Kultara walking-tour context and is a differentiator, not a nicety.
  • Bilingual: ID/EN toggle in profile; story text needs both from launch.
