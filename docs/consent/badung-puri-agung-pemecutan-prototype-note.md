# Prototype inclusion note — Puri Agung Pemecutan

**Place id:** `badung-puri-agung-pemecutan`
**Consent record:** `challange-5/Packages/Kultara/Sources/ContentKit/Content/consent/badung-puri-agung-pemecutan.json`
**Model:** D1-b — self-grant for an academic prototype (plan `c1-badung-single-quest-content.plan.md`, §E0)

## What this document is

This is **not** a permission granted by the management, custodians, or owners of Puri Agung Pemecutan.
No approach has been made to them, and no reply has been received.

It is a written statement by the project team that:

1. Puri Agung Pemecutan is named and included in a non-public academic prototype build of this app.
2. The scope of that inclusion is limited to **inclusion** and **naming** — the place appears in a
   walking route and is referred to by its real name. (`badung-catur-muka` additionally carries
   **photography**, because the quest offers a photo task at a public street monument there.)
3. No imagery of the site is bundled with the app.
4. The record exists so the build-time validator (rules V4 and V5, `NFR-GOV-01/02/03/07`) has a
   resolvable, non-fabricated record to check. `grantingBody` names the team, not the site, so the
   record can never be mistaken for a real grant.

## What must happen before anything public

- A real approach to the site's management, logged in `docs/consent-log.md`.
- Their reply, stored here as a scan or photograph.
- The consent record rewritten with the real granting body and a real named signatory, or the place
  removed from the manifest.
